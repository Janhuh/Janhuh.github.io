Our mission.

The State of Pet Homelessness Project' reveals there's approximately 362 million homeless pets across 20 countries surveyed. The findings indicate approximately 35% of cats and dogs live on the streets or in shelters. That's about 143 million dogs and 203 million cats on the streets.

It is also estimated that each day 1.2 million puppies are being born worldwide. The number of pups born each day overtakes the number of human babies born into the world, with approximately 1.2 million pups to 360,000 babies. That's roughly 3 times as many puppies as humans!

Our mission is to help as many homeless animals as we possible can. This is also why we take neutering very seriously in our shelter. By neutering 700 street dogs in the spawn of 12 years, this shelter has potentially prevented around 70,000 puppies from being born into dangerous, hungry and homeless lives.
