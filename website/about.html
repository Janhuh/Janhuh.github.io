THE BEGINNING OF HOPEPUPPIES

It all began with one dog in Bangkok.

While traveling through Thailand, Hanna (our founder) was overwhelmed by the number of sick, injured and forgotten dogs roaming the streets.
But it wasn’t until she met Roggo, a timid, starving dog hiding under a bench, that everything changed. She couldn’t walk away. She fed him, treated his wounds, and gave him safety. In return, Roggo gave her purpose.

Hanna couldn’t help them all. But she could start somewhere.

She decided to built a small shelter in Bangkok and another one in Austin, United States. With the help of a few dedicated volunteers, she began rescuing, healing, and rehoming street dogs—one by one. That was 12 years ago.

Today, our shelter has given over 1,200 dogs a second chance at life.
But it all started with just one.
Roggo.

And it continues—one dog at a time.
