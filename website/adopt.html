Happy to see you're interested in adopting a dog!

Adopting a dog is a lifelong commitment, not just a kind gesture.
We want every dog to find a safe, loving, and permanent home. To ensure that, we have a few important guidelines:

1. A Stable and Safe Home

  • You must be at least 21 years old to adopt.
  • We only adopt to homes where dogs are allowed (no temporary housing or lease violations).
  • A secure yard or regular leashed walks are required, no dogs will be adopted to homes where they’ll be left tied up outside.

2. Time, Patience, and Love

  • Many of our dogs come from difficult backgrounds. Adopters must be patient, especially in the early weeks.
  • Accidents, fear or shyness might be present at first. It takes time for a dog to adjust to a new life.

3. Long-Term Commitment

  • Dogs live 10–15 years or more. Please consider your future plans moving, kids, travel and work before adopting.
  • If your situation changes, you must contact us before rehoming the dog elsewhere.

4. No Chain Adoptions

  • You may not adopt a dog as a gift or on behalf of someone else. The adopter must be the one who will live with and care for the dog.

5. Other Pets

  • If you have other pets, we’ll help arrange a safe introduction. Some dogs don’t do well with cats or other dogs. Let’s find the right match for everyone.

6. Adoption Fee

  • An adoption fee applies. This helps cover vaccinations, neutering/spaying, microchipping and care while in the shelter.

We care about every dog like they’re our own.

If you’re ready to commit, we’ll help you find the perfect companion.
